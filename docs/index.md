# Brandon Calderon Morales
Engineer. Marine veteran. Omaha, Nebraska. English and Spanish.
{ .faded }

I pull at a log line like a loose thread until the whole knot makes
sense. I wanted to work with computers before I knew it was a job.
Somewhere along the way, the Marines gave me court-reporter typing
speed. Engineering gave me somewhere to put it.

I like small tools, clear explanations, and teaching whoever is next
to me. If I figure something out, I want the next person to have an
easier time of it.

## The day job

I lead technical delivery: helping engineering teams untangle security
findings, see how their systems are doing, and make shipping less
painful. A lot of that work is getting people and tools to make sense
to each other.

## Lately

- Family time — my wife, our two kids, and the extended fam
- Building little terminal tools with AI
- Kaggle and undergrad at night. Still a student.

## Been around

Nebraska is home. I've taken the long way around: across the States,
over to Spain, Germany, and Gibraltar, and back to the Midwest.
