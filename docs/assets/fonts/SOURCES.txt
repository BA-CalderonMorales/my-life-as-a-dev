# Local notebook fonts

Playpen Sans and JetBrains Mono are distributed under their included SIL Open Font License files. The unmodified Latin and Latin Extended WOFF2 variable-font subsets were downloaded from the official Google Fonts CSS2 service. JetBrains Mono and the extended Playpen Sans subset use variable files. Core Latin handwriting uses separate static weights to prioritize the visible face.

- `jetbrains-mono-latin-ext.woff2`: https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/jetbrainsmono/v24/tDbv2o-flEEny0FZhsfKu5WU4zr3E_BX0PnT8RD8yKwBNntkaToggR7BYRbKPx7cwhsk.woff2
- `jetbrains-mono-latin.woff2`: https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/jetbrainsmono/v24/tDbv2o-flEEny0FZhsfKu5WU4zr3E_BX0PnT8RD8yKwBNntkaToggR7BYRbKPxDcwg.woff2
- `playpen-sans-latin-ext.woff2`: https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/playpensans/v22/dg4i_pj1p6gXP0gzAZgm4c89QiIjuyw.woff2
