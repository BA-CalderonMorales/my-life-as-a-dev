# Additional Topics

Keep these saved for quick refreshers. All are free to read/watch.

## Must-read articles

- [The Joel Test: 12 Steps to Better Code](https://www.joelonsoftware.com/2000/08/09/the-joel-test-12-steps-to-better-code/) — a pragmatic scorecard for engineering teams.
- [Continuous Integration](https://martinfowler.com/articles/continuousIntegration.html) by Martin Fowler — foundational CI practices and pitfalls.
- [The Forest and the Desert](https://apenwarr.ca/log/20201227) — thinking about system design trade-offs and simplicity.

## Videos and channels

- [Anthropic Engineering YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Anthropic) — talks and demos on applied AI safety and tooling.
- [GOTO Conferences](https://www.youtube.com/@GOTO) — broad, practical engineering talks with a strong back catalog.

## Bonus references

- [12-Factor App](https://12factor.net/) — concise principles for building portable, maintainable services.
- [Google SRE Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/table-of-contents/) — free, battle-tested reliability patterns.
- [AWS Builders’ Library](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/) — deep dives on distributed systems techniques.
