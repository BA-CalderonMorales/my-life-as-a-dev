---
description: Ensure pages contain right content
---

Landing page should not display:
- Left-hand side bar menu
- Right-hand table of contents
- Footer generated by MkDocs

Landing page should display:
- GitHub repo information (typically located on top-right hand side next to search
- Main content generated by docs/index.md

Remaining pages should not display:
- Footer generated by MkDocs

Remaining pages should display:
- Left-hand side bar menu
- Right-hand table of contents

Clicking on the home page and then clicking on the other pages via the sticky tabs should not cause the expected content to disappear.
